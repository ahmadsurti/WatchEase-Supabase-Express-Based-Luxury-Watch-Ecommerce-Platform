The Chronicle

How a lunch changed everything.

Two developers. One hackathon. An Italian with a very interesting watch.

Scroll to read
I The Hackathon

We were two developers on the same team at a hackathon — Ahmad and Tanvir. The kind of event where you survive on bad coffee and the quiet belief that something interesting might happen.

Something interesting happened.

II The Encounter
"That's a beautiful watch."

Tanvir noticed it first. The sponsor of the event — an Italian gentleman — was wearing something extraordinary on his wrist. A conversation started. Turns out, he wasn't just a businessman. He was one of the most respected independent watch designers in Europe.

III The Lunch Table

Lunch break. Ahmad joined the table. Mr. Italian Watch Guy — as we still call him — leaned forward and started talking about an idea he'd been sitting on for years.

A closed, curated digital community for luxury timepieces. Not a marketplace. Not a catalogue. Something with weight. Something that felt like the room you walk into when you buy a serious watch.

IV The Proposition
"I have the pieces.
I have the network.
I need the world to see them."

He had designer connections across his network. What he needed was the digital side — the website, the brand identity, the hype, the shipping infrastructure. He asked us to build it.

We said yes before dessert arrived.

V What We Built

WatchEase is the result. A digital space that carries the weight of a premium showroom — without the four walls. Every piece curated. Every detail considered. Built by two people who take both code and craft seriously.

We believe the click of a button should feel as momentous as the winding of a crown.
